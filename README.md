## Roster & Core Policy

CrossfireGG does not enforce a full roster lock during the regular season or group stage. Teams may adjust players prior to playoffs, subject to tournament administration approval.

All teams must maintain a consistent Team Core (3 players) for competitive integrity.

### VRS Teams

- Must maintain at least three (3) players from the matched VRS roster (August 3, 2026 snapshot) at all times
- Changing one or two players does not affect VRS eligibility
- Falling below three snapshot players results in immediate disqualification to preserve VRS integrity

### Non-VRS Teams

- Must also maintain a consistent Core-3 identity for competitive integrity
- Administrative review may occur if the core identity changes

### Duplicate Core Restrictions

- **Duplicate core rosters are not allowed** — no two teams may share 3 or more core players
- Teams found in violation will be disqualified
- This rule ensures roster integrity across the competitive landscape

### Coach Substitution Rule

- Registered team coaches are permitted to substitute into matches as players, provided they are listed on the official roster prior to match start and meet all eligibility requirements.

## Tournament Format

### Group Stage

- **Format:** Round-Robin Group Stage
- **Structure:** 12 Groups × 4 Teams (48 teams total; the 8 highest-seeded VRS teams bye directly to Playoffs and are not placed in a group)
- **Match Type:** All matches are Best-of-3 (BO3)
- **Duration:** August 31 – September 8, 2026
- **Advancement:** Top 2 teams from each group advance to Playoffs (24 qualifiers), joining the 8 bye teams for a 32-team Playoff field
- **Default Match Day:** The middle day of each round window
- Teams may reschedule matches within their assigned round window with mutual agreement

**Match Rounds:**

- Round 1: August 31 – September 2
- Round 2: September 3 – September 5
- Round 3: September 6 – September 8

**Seeding Break:** September 9 – September 11

### Seeding Method

Teams are seeded using a **VRS-first, record-second methodology**:

1. **VRS-matched teams** (18 teams) — a team is matched to a Valve Regional Standings (Americas) roster when 3 or more of its signed-up players (a "core") also appear on that VRS entry's roster. VRS-matched teams are seeded ahead of all others, ordered by VRS standing (best to worst).
2. **Top 8 seeds bye directly to Playoffs.** The 8 best VRS-matched teams do not play the Group Stage.
3. **Remaining teams are seeded by ESEA League Division**, best to worst: Advanced > Main > Intermediate > Open (Group 9-10) > Open (Group 1-8), then by regular-season record/placement within each tier.
4. **Fresh Teams** — teams with no VRS match and no qualifying ESEA record — are seeded last, below every ranked team.
5. **Seeds 9–56** (the 10 remaining VRS teams + all ESEA-tier + Fresh teams) are distributed across the 12 groups using a snake-seeding pattern, so every group draws from a mix of seeding bands.

Full team-by-team seeding is published in [Teams and Rankings](TEAMS.md).

### Playoff Stage

- **Format:** 32-Team Double Elimination Bracket
- **Field:** 24 teams qualify through the Group Stage (top 2 per group) + 8 top-seeded teams bye directly in
- **Match Type:** All matches are Best-of-3 (BO3)
- **Grand Final:** Best-of-5 (BO5) — the Winner's Bracket Champion begins the Grand Final with a 1-0 map advantage
- **Duration:** September 12 – October 11, 2026
- **Default Match Day:** The middle day of each playoff window
- Matches may be rescheduled within each round window with admin approval

**Playoff Rounds:**

- WB Round of 32: September 12 – September 14
- WB Round of 16 + Losers Round 1: September 15 – September 17
- WB Quarterfinals + Losers Round 2: September 18 – September 20
- Losers Round 3: September 21 – September 23
- WB Semifinals + Losers Round 4: September 24 – September 26
- Losers Round 5: September 27 – September 29
- WB Final + Losers Round 6: September 30 – October 2
- Losers Round 7: October 3 – October 5
- Losers Final: October 6 – October 8
- Grand Final (BO5): October 9 – October 11

## Season 6 vs Season 5 — Key Changes

### Prize Pool & Entry

| Item | Season 5 | Season 6 |
|------|----------|----------|
| Entry Fee | $150 | **$200** |
| Prize Pool | $5,000 | **$10,000** |
| Teams | 32 | **56** |

### Format & Structure

- **Group Stage:** Season 5 was 8 groups of 4 (32 teams); Season 6 is **12 groups of 4 (48 teams)**, with the **top 8 VRS-seeded teams byeing directly to Playoffs**
- **Playoffs:** Season 5 was a 16-team Single Elimination bracket; Season 6 is a **32-team Double Elimination bracket** (24 group qualifiers + 8 direct byes)
- **Grand Final:** New for Season 6 — Best-of-5, with the Winner's Bracket Champion starting 1-0

### Seeding / VRS

- **Season 5:** Used the May 4, 2026 VRS snapshot; VRS matching was based on teams appearing on both the roster list and the VRS leaderboard
- **Season 6:** Uses the **August 3, 2026 VRS snapshot**; VRS status is established by a formal **core-of-3 shared-player match** against each VRS roster entry, so org/branding changes between seasons don't cost a team its VRS status

### Schedule

- **Season Length:** Season 5 ran ~6 weeks; Season 6 runs **~6 weeks** (August 31 – October 11), with a longer, 10-round Playoff bracket due to the Double Elimination format
- **Seeding Break:** September 9–11 (in place of Season 5's Break Week)
