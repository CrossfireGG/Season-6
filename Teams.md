## Seeding Notes

### VRS Seeding Priority

- **All 56 teams entered through fully open registration** — no teams were invited. Valve Regional Standings (VRS) was applied afterward, purely as a seeding input, to preserve VRS integrity for teams that already qualified by signing up
- Teams matching a Valve Regional Standings (VRS) Americas roster are seeded **first**, ordered by VRS standing (best to worst)
- VRS status requires a **core of 3+ shared players** between a team's signed-up Season 6 roster (Starters + Subs) and a single VRS roster entry — organization/branding name is not used for matching, since orgs can rebrand between seasons
- Where a team's registered name differs from the VRS org name it matched (roster overlap confirmed), the VRS org name is noted above — the *players*, not the name, establish VRS status
- The **top 8 VRS-seeded teams** receive a bye directly to Playoffs (see Playoff Bye Teams above) and do not play in the Group Stage — this is a seeding outcome of open registration, not a separate invite

### Non-VRS Teams

- Remaining teams are seeded by **ESEA Season 57 division tier**, best to worst: **Advanced > Main > Intermediate > Open (Group 9-10) > Open (Group 1-8)**, then by regular-season placement (best to worst) within each tier
- **Fresh Teams** — teams with no VRS match and no Season 57 ESEA record — are seeded last, below every ranked team. 
### Tournament Lock Notice

To ensure **fair play and competitive integrity** for all participants, seeding has been finalized prior to Round 1, after which no further seeding or team changes will be made.

## Team Requirements

### Roster Rules

- **Maximum roster:** 8 players (5 starters, 2 substitutes, 1 coach)
- **Minimum core players:** 3 original core players must play per match
- **Roster changes:** Allowed during Group Stage, locked for Playoffs
- **All players must reside in North America**

### Eligibility

- ✅ FACEIT Anti-Cheat required
- ✅ No active VAC or platform bans
- ✅ Team captain designated from core players
- ✅ Entry fee: $200 per team
- ✅ Payment due immediately after registration submission
