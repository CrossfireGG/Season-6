# Teams & Rankings — Season 6

This teams file will be populated once registration completes and the VRS snapshot used for seeding is published.

## VRS Direct Playoff Invites
- 8 teams will receive direct playoff invites based on the announced VRS snapshot and Crossfire invitation policy.

## Group Stage Composition
- 48 teams will compete in the Group Stage divided into 12 groups of 4 teams each.
- Top 2 teams from each group advance to playoffs (24 teams), joining the 8 VRS invites to form a 32-team double-elimination playoff bracket.

## Seeding Notes
- VRS-ranked teams are seeded first based on the announced VRS snapshot used for this season.
- Remaining teams will be seeded using ESEA division and recent performance metrics.

## Publish Plan
Once registration closes and VRS seeds are confirmed we will publish the full seeded team list and group assignments here and on Discord.
